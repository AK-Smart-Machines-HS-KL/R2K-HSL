** Background of this project **
Information dimensions: the curse of modalities
- spatial (static, dynamic): world model
- (written) natural language: knowlege (eg rules of the game, samples for expert and oracle)
- physics: noise, jitter
- time
- actions: eg move, kick, stand: enduring or short

Reasoning problems
- frame problem: the domain is non-deterministic, heuristic reasoning is necessary
- partially explorable: team reds reactions and refeere decions are not 100%  predictable in some world states

So information must be  transformed or transduced or discretized to enhance the small LLMs comprehension and reasoning
- provide a canonical language
- use runtime algorithms to engineer incoming information

New Approach in the sense of :
* ros2k must optimize the following chain of data and control, from (a) to (d) *

(a) sensing: getting a reliable world model (w/o prediction, w/o yaw, past frame data etc).)

(b) analysis: understanding soccer game world state incl. apropriate data (see chain (a)), rules (see auto referee) and limitations (spatial like field dimensions, physical conditions like max speed, deceleration of ball )

(c) expert (oracle): suggesting a winning strategy, which spans from seconds to minutes, is based upon experience, using role assignments for structured and tactical scripts (eg striker is a dynamic role, goali may be static, depending on rules)

(d) team-captain (the forthcomming c3 module): mapping strategy to physical actions (eg move, kick). may require a redesign of the ROS2K bridge, but will always be based on ROS2 communication and tools.

we need to define and optimize the communication between the layers, and provide information (data in context).

Keys:
- develop a brief, expressive, semantically rich intermediate language, that will be
  understoof by 3B model, team-captain, and human user.

- remove contradictive, or wishy-washy argumentation (output in (b),(c)), as seen in the 3B model.
  Samples:
  "the ball is near opponents goal, but also near own goal",
  "prioritze defending while also attacking"
  "looking for an opportunity to attack"

- reduce latency within Ollama (if do-able): let 3B model become aware, whether analysis or
  strategy did'n change since last call, and then let the team captain
  simply follow the current stratey (ie set of actions). In this case, 3B model output is a short
  token like "continue". this goes beyond hashing the world mode (to reduce delay)

- monitor how to integrate the advantages of C1 (goal) and C9 ? why do they interfere?
