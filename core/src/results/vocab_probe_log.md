- **latency:** 926 ms
- **response:**

Zone defense is a defensive strategy where players cover specific areas of the field rather than sticking closely to their opponents, as they do with man-to-man marking. In this setup, defenders stay within their designated zone and work together more often than in a man-marking system. This allows for better support from teammates who are covering different parts of the pitch, but it can be less predictable for the offense.

Zone defense often provides a different kind of challenge, requiring players to communicate and coordinate effectively in order to maintain their zones and support teammates who are far away from them.
